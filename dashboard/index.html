    Not ranked: appeals with no needs data

    These countries have their own appeal, but the data source has no people-in-need figure for them, so a fair score is not possible. They are listed rather than hidden.

    Method and limits

    • Data: funding per appeal from OCHA FTS and people in need from HDX HAPI (humanitarian needs, intersectoral, national level). Funding is for the current year.
    • Score: each crisis gets a percentile rank on three measures, combined with the weights you set (default 40% funded, 40% dollars per person, 20% scale). 100 means the most neglected of the crises compared.
    • Relative, not absolute: a low score means better off than the others here, not that the need is met.
    • Regional response plans (refugee-hosting countries) are excluded because per-country funding is not reliable.
    • Older needs figures are flagged: the funding is for this year but the people-in-need number is older, so dollars per person is indicative only.
    • Dots show the funded share proportionally. Funding covers the whole appeal, not each person individually.
