Live

Ongoing disasters, alerts and outbreaks from four public sources, listed as they publish them. Nothing here is scored or ranked.

Sources. ReliefWeb (UN OCHA): disasters marked ongoing or alert. GDACS (EU and UN): Orange and Red alerts from the last 30 days. USGS: significant earthquakes of the past week. WHO: the latest 15 Disease Outbreak News notices. Each row links to the original. Severity words are the source's own, and a missing item means the source did not list it, not that nothing happened. Data is refreshed when python src/fetch_live.py is run; a daily automatic refresh is planned. Conflict and hunger data (ACLED, IPC) are not included yet.
