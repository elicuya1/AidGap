What is happening now.

Ongoing disasters, alerts and outbreaks, pulled from four public sources and listed as they publish them. This page does not score or rank them: the Neglect Score on the main page is a separate analysis.

Events

Sources. ReliefWeb (UN OCHA) disasters marked ongoing or alert; GDACS (EU and UN) Orange and Red alerts from the last 30 days; USGS significant earthquakes of the past week; WHO Disease Outbreak News (latest 15). Each card links to the original. Severity words are the source's own; a missing item means the source did not list it, not that nothing happened. Data is refreshed when python src/fetch_live.py is run; a daily automatic refresh is planned. Conflict events and hunger (ACLED, IPC) are not included yet.
