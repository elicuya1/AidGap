Events

Sources. ReliefWeb (UN OCHA) disasters marked ongoing or alert; GDACS (EU and UN) Orange and Red alerts from the last 30 days; USGS significant earthquakes of the past week; WHO Disease Outbreak News (latest 15); IFRC GO active Red Cross and Red Crescent appeals; NASA EONET open storms, volcanoes and wildfires over 10,000 acres. Each card links to the original. Severity words are the source's own; a missing item means the source did not list it, not that nothing happened. Grouping. Items from different sources are merged when they name the same first country and the same type of event within 30 days of each other. That can wrongly merge two separate floods in one country, so untick the box above to see every listing. Events in small places the map has no outline for are in the list but not on the map. The data file is rebuilt automatically about every 30 minutes by a GitHub Action, and this page re-reads it (and checks USGS directly) every 5 minutes while it is open. Conflict events and hunger (ACLED, IPC) are not included yet. New badges compare with what this browser saw on your last visit.
